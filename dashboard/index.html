How value is calculated

Every implied value comes from this snapshot's own figures — no list of names is typed in by hand.

implied value = TVL × category multiple × momentum × age factor

  • Category multiple — the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the tokenised protocols in the same category, measured from this snapshot. It is the market's own pricing of a tokenised protocol in that category. A category with fewer than three tokenised protocols falls back to the global median.
  • Momentum — the protocol's 7-day TVL change, clamped to ±30%. Growing TVL lifts the value; shrinking TVL cuts it.
  • Age factor — days since DefiLlama began tracking the protocol with no token, on a log scale capped at +30%. Longer at scale with no token means more pent-up launch value.

The board holds protocols that record no token. Protocols whose token already trades are not rows — they exist only to calibrate the multiples. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade are left off too: they are not launches waiting to happen.
